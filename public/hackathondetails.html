event description

CodeSprint is a 24-hour non-stop coding marathon where creativity meets code. Participants will tackle real-world problems, build software solutions, and pitch their ideas to a panel of industry experts.

Who Can Join?

Open to all Engineering Students (UG & PG) with a passion for tech, coding, or problem-solving.

rules & guidelines

  • Team Size: 2-4 members
  • Themes will be released at the start of the event
  • All coding must be done during the hackathon
  • Internet is allowed; plagiarism is not
  • Bring your own laptops and accessories
