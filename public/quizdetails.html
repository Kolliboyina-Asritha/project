event description

The Engineering Quiz Competition is a thrilling knowledge-based event where innovation meets intellect. This quiz challenges participants to push the boundaries of their engineering knowledge through a series of mind-bending questions and problem-solving tasks. Covering a wide spectrum of topics including core engineering concepts, emerging technologies, and real-world applications, the competition is structured in multiple rounds to test speed, accuracy, and teamwork.


Why Participate?


Enhance technical knowledge

Win prizes and certificates

Networking with peers and professionals

Boost confidence and academic profile

rules & guidelines

  • Each team must consist of 1 to 3 members
  • Only undergraduate engineering students are allowed to participate. Valid student ID is mandatory.
  • Use of mobile phones, calculators, or any external aids is strictly prohibited.
  • Any form of cheating or misconduct will result in immediate disqualification.
  • The quizmaster's and judge's decisions are final and binding.
