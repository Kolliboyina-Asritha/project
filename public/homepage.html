Our Event Planning Web Application is designed to simplify the entire event management process—from creating event pages to handling guest lists and RSVPs. Whether you're organizing a college fest, a workshop, or a casual get-together, our platform makes planning effortless and efficient.

Created by a team of passionate computer science students, this app was born out of the need to manage college events more effectively. With a clean, user-friendly interface and mobile-friendly access, it’s the perfect tool for students, coordinators, and event lovers alike.
