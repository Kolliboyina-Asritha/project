Sankranti Celebrations

Festival of Harvest and Joy

About Event

🔹Sankranthi is a major harvest festival celebrated in mid-January, especially in states like Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka. It marks the movement of the Sun into the Capricorn zodiac sign, bringing longer days and the end of winter. The festival is considered highly auspicious and joyful.

🔹The main reason for celebrating Sankranthi is to thank the Sun God and nature for a good harvest. It is a time of gratitude for farmers and communities, as crops like paddy, sugarcane, and turmeric are harvested during this period. The festival reflects the deep connection between people and the land.

🔹Sankranthi is usually celebrated over four days – Bhogi, Sankranthi, Kanuma, and Mukkanuma. Each day has special customs like lighting bonfires, flying colorful kites, drawing rangoli (muggulu), wearing traditional clothes, and preparing festive sweets like ariselu and chakralu

event description

Sankranti is one of the most vibrant festivals celebrated in our college with enthusiasm and tradition. Students wear colorful attire, participate in rangoli competitions, fly kites, and enjoy cultural performances that mark the beginning of the harvest season.

This festival brings families and friends together to celebrate with love and joy. It is a time to share, enjoy cultural traditions, and welcome a season of prosperity and happiness. Sankranthi beautifully blends nature, tradition, and togetherness into one vibrant celebration.
